Bark 0.2 brings a smoother sidebar, link previews, and native browser settings.

- Swipe or click between Spaces with an animated transition. Hover over a Space name to collapse its pinned tabs or open Space settings.
- Open links from pinned tabs in Peek. Dismiss the preview, turn it into a regular tab, or open it alongside the current page in Split View.
- Close a pinned tab’s session and keep the pin in the sidebar. Reopen the pin when you need it again.
- Open the address popover with Command-T and add a sidebar tab by navigating. Opening a link from another app goes straight to its destination.
- Customize Bark in the native Settings window and set each Space’s appearance from the sidebar.
- Copy the current page URL with Command-Shift-C and get a confirmation. Bark also removes the stock New Tab promotions and the vertical-tabs and Split View tutorials.

Signed with Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 13 or later. Move Bark.app to Applications before launching. Existing installations can update through **Bark → Check for Updates…**.
